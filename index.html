01WHY AEA EXISTS

Who we are

The room where
disciplines collide.

AEA Club is a multidisciplinary engineering community for students who want more than a syllabus.

We connect mechanical minds with software builders, circuit hackers with civil thinkers, and every curious engineer in between. Through projects, workshops, mentorship, and a little healthy chaos, we make room for ideas to become real.
